1. Executive summary

  1. Growth is entirely volume-driven. Revenue/day rose from $2,635 (Jan) to $5,550 (Jun). Line items/day went 559 → 1,178, while average line value stayed at $4.65–4.72 and average unit price at ~$3.38. Prices and basket value did not move; more transactions did. Chắc Chắn
  2. The three stores are near-identical. Each holds 33–34% of revenue, grew at the same rate every month, and has the same category mix (Coffee 38.6–38.7% in every store). The real differences are when they trade, not how much. Chắc Chắn
  3. Morning is the business. 7–11am brings 46% of revenue. Lower Manhattan relies on mornings most (57% of its revenue before 11am); Astoria least (39%), with steady trade until close. Chắc Chắn
  4. Long tail. 43 of 80 products make 80% of revenue. The bottom 10 together earn under $14k over six months, about 2% of the total. Chắc Chắn
  5. Day of week doesn't matter. Average revenue/day ranges only $3,782 (Sun) to $3,911 (Mon), a 3% spread. Weekend promotions have no demand gap to fill. Chắc Chắn
Read this before acting on the results. (a) There is no basket or receipt ID: each row is one product line, so "$4.69" is the value per line, not per customer visit, and attach rates or basket analysis can't be measured. (b) There is no cost, staffing or margin data, so the evidence covers revenue only, not profit. (c) Store shares this even, identical monthly growth and flat weekdays are unusual for real retail. This looks like a synthetic or teaching dataset (it matches the public Maven Analytics "Coffee Shop Sales" set). Treat the store comparisons as an exercise, not as proof about real stores. 5. Data quality

  • No missing values and no duplicate transaction IDs. Chắc Chắn
  • 4 made-to-order drinks have no size in their name: "Latte" ($3.75) sits next to "Latte Rg" ($4.25), "Cappuccino" ($3.75) next to "Cappuccino Lg" ($4.25), plus "Espresso shot" and "Ouro Brasileiro shot". The model labels them "Unlabelled". They are top sellers (Latte alone ≈ $17k), so size-level results are approximate. Chắc Chắn
  • 15 products have more than one unit price. Most differences are cents (bakery ±$0.02), but the T-shirt ranges $23–28 and Organic Decaf $22.50–28. This could be a price change or a data entry issue. Ước Đoán
  • Quantity per line is 1–2 in 98% of rows. There are 13 lines with qty 6–8; these look like group orders, not errors. Có Khả Năng Cao
